NICU Fluids Calculator

Instructions

To Calculate Full Feeding:
Select Full Feeding, then enter the milk type and feeding interval. The feed volume per feed will be automatically calculated based on the target fluid entered. PN, lipid and IVD inputs will be ignored and are not required.

To Calculate Increment Feeding or NBM:
Please complete BOTH the PN and IVD sections. If PN, lipid or IVD are not being used, please enter 0 instead of leaving the field blank.

For Increment Feeding:
Enter the milk type, feed volume and feeding interval.

For NBM:
Milk type, feed volume and feeding interval will be ignored.

IVD:
If IVD rate is more than 0, please enter drip type, dextrose concentration and KCl per pint. If no KCl is added, please enter 0.
